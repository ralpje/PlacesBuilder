How it works

From floor plan to Places map in three steps.

STEP 1

Add your floor plan

Create an office with its floors and upload a plan per floor. Place, scale and rotate it on the satellite map.

STEP 2

Match rooms to Places

Import your Places export and draw each room on the plan. Every room is linked to its place in Microsoft Places.

STEP 3

Download and import

Download the IMDF package and import it into Microsoft Places yourself. This app changes nothing in your Places tenant.

Questions, answered

Frequently asked questions

Do I need admin rights to use it?

Only once. The first person from your organisation to sign in needs an administrator to approve the app. After that, colleagues can be invited into the workspace without further approval.

Is it free?

Yes. The project does not charge for the service; it relies on voluntary donations. The beta comes without a service level and can change as it proceeds.

Does it change anything in Microsoft Places?

No. The app produces an IMDF package. You import it into Microsoft Places yourself.

Can I run it myself?

Yes. It is open source under the AGPL-3.0 licence and deploys to your own Azure subscription.

Ready to try it?

This is an early version. Feedback is welcome through a GitHub issue. Please do not post floor plans or tenant data there.
